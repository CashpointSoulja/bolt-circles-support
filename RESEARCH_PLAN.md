# Research plan (planned, not completed)

No research has been carried out for this prototype. There are no interview findings and no quotes. Everything below is a plan.

## Goals
1. Do people understand exactly what a supporter will see from the preview?
2. Do they trust that removing access really removes it?
3. What kind of support feels helpful, and what makes someone stop?
4. Can the flow be completed with a keyboard and a screen reader?

## Participants
- Five participants and five supporters, recruited voluntarily.
- Compensation handled outside the prototype, only if separately approved.
- Nobody is asked about medication, diagnosis, weight or treatment. If someone volunteers it, the facilitator doesn't record it.

## Sessions (about 45 minutes each)
1. **Sharing comfort.** Who, if anyone, would you ask? What would you never want them to see?
2. **Preview comprehension.** Show the review screen, hide it, ask the person to list what the supporter sees. Pass = all fields named, nothing extra assumed.
3. **Revocation trust.** Remove access, then open the old link as the supporter. Ask what the supporter can still see and how confident they are.
4. **Helpful support.** Supporters choose from the preset notes. Ask what was missing and whether anything felt like pressure.
5. **Reasons to stop.** What would make you leave or mute?
6. **Care entry.** Does the "Explore clinician-led care" card feel separate from the circle and easy to skip?

## Accessibility
- Keyboard-only run through every path at 390 px and desktop widths.
- Screen reader run (VoiceOver on iOS, NVDA on Windows).
- Contrast check against WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Review before any real pilot
- A clinician and a privacy specialist review all boundary and safety copy.
- Privacy, legal and clinical sign-off; informed consent; secure authorisation; a retention policy and an incident process.

## Outputs (to be produced)
- A short findings note with counts (for example, "4 of 5 named every field"), without invented quotes.
- Copy and flow changes, then a re-test of preview comprehension.
