# What this does
A person can ask one trusted person for support without sharing their medication or private health details. They choose what to share, see the invitation first, and can turn it off at any time. Their friend can say something supportive or join a simple activity. No one gives medical advice.

The second screen asks whether it really helped people come back. It does not count every click as success or push people to buy medicine. Everything is made-up demo data. The useful proof is a working invitation/privacy journey plus an honest experiment decision.
